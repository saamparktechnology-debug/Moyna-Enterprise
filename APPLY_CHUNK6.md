# Chunk 6 (updated) - advance receipt, and invoice / credit note / receipt in the layout of your printed invoice

Unzip over the project root (paths are relative to it), after chunks 1 to 5. No migration.

    pnpm build:packages

Then open Settings > Company and fill in: Company name, Proprietor, GSTIN, Registered address, Phone, Tagline and Invoice terms.
They make up the heading and foot of every invoice, credit note and receipt.
